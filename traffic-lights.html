Confidence Indicators

Performance Property Advisory · Data to Q3 2026
Confidence is median days on market, so it splits by house and unit: green at 16 days or fewer, amber above. It reproduces 25 of the 26 house and unit verdicts in the reference set. There is no red band — the reference contains none, so nothing in it says where red would start. Supply & Demand reads each segment straight off the demand-score bands (green ≥ 30, amber 10–29, red < 10) — 30 is where rent growth matches CPI, 10 is flat rents. Value reads the affordability runway band (green ≥ 65%, amber 40–64%, red < 40%), shifted a step when the price ranking sits well away from its own long-run average. Neither pillar is scored. Demand score is built on Cotality vacancy and rent (the V2 basis), September 2026; runway is basis-independent and the price ranking is annual, reading January 2026. Forecasts are set aside for now.
