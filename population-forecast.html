Population Forecast

How many people each market is expected to add each year to 2028-29: births minus deaths, moves within Australia and overseas migration, plus residents from major projects in the markets that have them. The Major projects view shows the projects behind that figure. Built on the same inputs as the Vacancy Rate Projection, so the two agree.
