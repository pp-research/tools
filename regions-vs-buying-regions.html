Growth uses Cotality's 12-month median sale price for each council area (capitals: Greater Capital City area). Markets with fewer than 100 sales a year are left out. "No 5% fall since 2000" means the 12-month median never fell 5% from a high in that time. Small markets can show jumps from a handful of sales. In the charts, the short break in mid-2018 is where Cotality changed its data series; capital cities can show a small step there.

2 · Rent growth vs price growth

The gap is rent growth minus price growth over the same period, in percentage points. A positive gap means rents rose faster than prices, so rental yields widened and the market got cheaper relative to its rent. That's the "prices stagnant while rents keep rising" pattern. A negative gap means prices outran rents. Each cell shows the gap, with the rent and price changes behind it underneath. Columns are the period: 1 year to 5 years.
