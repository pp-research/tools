          Method & sources

          • Regions
            The six metropolitan regions of the Victorian Government’s Plan Melbourne 2017–2050: Inner Metro, Inner South-East, Eastern, Southern, Northern and Western — 31 LGAs. The ABS Greater Melbourne GCCSA also takes in parts of Macedon Ranges, Mitchell and Murrindindi; those LGAs are not in a Plan Melbourne metro region and are left out.
          • Same deck as Sydney
            Every exhibit, rule and threshold follows the Greater Sydney breakdown. Pages whose Victorian source is not yet wired are marked Pending VIC data and show no figures.
          • Price level, not median
            A region is not a Cotality geography, so no region median is published. The figure shown is the sales-weighted average of member-LGA medians, each a real Cotality median. The min–max range is the honest signal of internal spread.
          • Composite figures
            Exactly the Sydney report’s rules, re-verified on its own data (878 of 878 fields reproduced): sales-weighted for medians, days on market, discount and growth; rental-listing-weighted for rents; property-weighted for yield and hold period; stock on market as a ratio of sums; simple sums for counts.
          • LGA names
            Moreland is Merri-bek from 2022; the 2023+ workbooks tag Bayside and Kingston “(Vic.)”. Both are normalised so every vintage carries all 31 LGAs.
          • Live data
            Cotality Market Trends, LGA and capital-city tabs, nine vintages December 2018 – May 2026. Boundaries: ABS ASGS 2021 LGA, dissolved by region.
          • Still required
            See VIC context for the three Victorian sources still to collect. ABS population, approvals, SALM, IVI and lending data are held and are being wired in page by page.
